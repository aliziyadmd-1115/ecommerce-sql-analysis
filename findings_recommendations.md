# Findings and Business Recommendations

## Stakeholder
E-Commerce Sales & Operations Manager

## Business Objective
The purpose of this analysis is to evaluate marketplace sales performance,
customer behavior, product categories, sellers, payments, delivery performance,
and customer satisfaction to identify opportunities for growth and operational
improvement.

# Key Findings

## 1. Marketplace Sales Performance
The marketplace completed 96,478 delivered orders, generating approximately
$13.22 million in product GMV. Average order value was $137.04, while total
freight charges were approximately $2.20 million.

Sales increased substantially throughout 2017 and remained relatively strong
during 2018. November 2017 had the highest delivered-order volume with 7,289
orders, while May 2018 generated the highest monthly GMV at $977,544.69.

## 2. Product Category Performance
Health & Beauty generated the highest overall product GMV at approximately
$1.23 million. Watches & Gifts followed at $1.17 million, while Bed, Bath &
Table had the highest delivered-order volume with 9,272 orders.

Several major categories also experienced strong year-over-year growth when
comparing January-August 2017 with January-August 2018. Health & Beauty added
approximately $512,203 in GMV, Watches & Gifts added $486,717, and Sports &
Leisure added $300,442.

## 3. Customer Retention
Only 2,801 of 93,358 customers with delivered orders purchased more than once,
resulting in a repeat-customer rate of approximately 3.00%.

This indicates that repeat purchasing represents a significant opportunity for
the marketplace.

## 4. Geographic Performance
São Paulo was the marketplace's largest geographic market, producing
approximately $5.07 million in GMV and accounting for 38.33% of total
delivered-order GMV.

São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais together generated approximately
63.38% of GMV, showing substantial geographic concentration.

## 5. Delivery Performance and Customer Satisfaction
Delivery performance showed a strong relationship with customer satisfaction.

On-time deliveries received an average review score of 4.29, compared with
only 2.57 for late deliveries.

Late-delivery rates also varied considerably by state. Alagoas had the highest
rate among states analyzed at 23.93%, followed by Maranhão at 19.67%.

## 6. Payment Behavior
Credit cards were the dominant payment method, representing 73.92% of payment
records and approximately $12.54 million in payment value.

Credit-card purchases also averaged 3.51 installments, while the other major
payment methods averaged approximately one installment.

## 7. Seller Concentration
The highest-performing seller generated approximately $226,988 in GMV but
represented only 1.72% of total marketplace GMV.

The top 10 sellers together accounted for approximately 13.29% of GMV,
suggesting that marketplace sales were distributed across many sellers rather
than being dominated by a small group.

# Business Recommendations

## 1. Improve Customer Retention
With only a 3.00% repeat-customer rate, the marketplace should investigate
retention strategies such as personalized promotions, post-purchase marketing,
loyalty incentives, and recommendations based on previous purchases.

## 2. Prioritize Delivery Improvements
Late deliveries were associated with substantially lower customer ratings.
Operations teams should investigate fulfillment and transportation issues,
particularly in states with high late-delivery rates such as Alagoas, Maranhão,
Piauí, Ceará, and Rio de Janeiro.

## 3. Invest in High-Growth Categories
Categories such as Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts, Sports & Leisure,
Computers & Accessories, Baby, and Housewares showed strong GMV growth.
Marketplace management should consider increasing promotional support and
seller recruitment in these areas.

## 4. Investigate Low-Satisfaction Categories
Office Furniture had the lowest average review score among categories with a
meaningful review sample. Product quality, seller performance, packaging, and
delivery issues within this category should be investigated.

## 5. Balance Geographic Growth
Because a large share of GMV comes from São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas
Gerais, the marketplace should protect performance in these key markets while
evaluating opportunities to expand demand and improve logistics in
underrepresented states.
